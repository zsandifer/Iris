Iris privacy policy

Iris is a personal AI agent that runs on its owner's own computer. This copy of Iris is operated privately by its owner for their own use.

What Iris stores

Chats, memory, settings, and the data Iris reads from services you connect (for example Gmail, Calendar, Drive and YouTube) are stored only on the owner's computer. Connection tokens and saved logins are kept encrypted.

How it is used

Iris uses your data only to do what you ask and to show you things you might like. It acts on your behalf only within the rules you set, and asks before sending, buying, deleting, or sharing anything.

Sharing

Iris does not sell or share your data with anyone. To answer you, Iris sends the relevant text to the AI service its owner has signed in to (such as Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's ChatGPT), under that service's terms. There is no analytics or tracking.

Google user data

Iris's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Google at any time from Iris's Connections screen or from your Google Account's security settings, and delete Iris's copy by removing the connection.

Contact

Questions go to the owner of this Iris, at the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen.

Iris is open source: github.com/zsandifer/Iris.
